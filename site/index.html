How the fare is worked out

  1. Pick up. List completed runs in the window from /actions/runs (works on public and private repos, any plan).
  2. Every leg. Pull all jobs of each run, including earlier attempts, so re-runs show up as their own cost.
  3. Tariff. Read each job's runner labels: Linux, Windows, macOS, arm, self-hosted, and size from N-cores.
  4. Round up. Each job is billed in whole minutes. A 40-second lint job is a 1-minute fare.
  5. Receipt. Rank workflows and jobs by cost, show the re-run share, optionally fail CI over a budget.

Get in

CLI (Python 3.9+, no dependencies; uses GH_TOKEN or your gh login):

pipx install git+https://github.com/Blackman99/runbill
runbill OWNER/REPO --days 30

Or as a step that writes the receipt to the job summary:

- uses: Blackman99/runbill@v0.1.0
  with:
    days: 30
    fail-over: 50   # optional budget gate, USD
